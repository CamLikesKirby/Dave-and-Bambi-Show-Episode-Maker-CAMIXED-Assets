(NOTICE: Expunged uses any pronouns)

Expunged is viewed as the number one danger of Frust. Pretty much every Frustian knows about her and fears him.
He doesn't really have an ultimate goal and is a little tunnel visioned with his takeover. She gains power via killing other entities. This would have had to require an already huge amount of power to first achieve. Expunged attempted to quicken this process by making minions of her own who assist him in gaining power. This resulted in varying success, and eventually evolved into her creating killing machines, those being the Trix (Species). The more sentient batch of minions, she seemed to almost take a liking to and try and make friends with. This was until an incident of betrayal which pushed her away from such.
Expunged used to hunt for prey themselves but lost the need to as his vast army makes it almost obsolete.
He will still go out and about and scare people just for fun though, often not even killing them.
To protect one from expunged would require an entity with the same powers as her.
