Brobgonal is an ex Expunged minion. He was originally compliant in helping Expunged gain power and take over Frust, because that's what he was born into.
He hung around a bad crowd in his time in the Bambi minion army. Brob wasn't accepted by other Bambi minions because of how abnormally short he was.
He attempted to make his own personality seem more cruel and uncaring to appeal to them.

Eventually, he decided to run away from the Expunged minion army.
While looking for a new place to stay he was offered a home by Barbu. Brobgonal was originally seen as just another minion. But once he had a chance to explain his situation, Barbu had more sympathy.
Brob told many stories about his old home. He was very thankful for Barbu's hospitality so he helped him around the house with things like cooking.
Barbu decided to let Brob stay and when enough time passed they fell in love.
